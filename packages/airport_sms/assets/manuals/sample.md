# Airport SMS Manual (sample)

## SMS-1.1 Hazard reporting

Field staff must report a hazard with location and evidence. Do not wait for a shift handover.

## SMS-2.3 ALARP

A case may move from assessing to verifying or closed without a new CAPA only when residual risk is ALARP.

## SMS-4.1 Close authority

Only the safety office may close a case. Reporters must not see close-case tools.
